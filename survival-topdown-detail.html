Survival Top Down

Unity 2022.3 LTS | C# | Mobile & PC Action Survival Prototype
Survival Top Down is a high-octane 3D action-survival combat prototype engineered in Unity and C#, supporting both Mobile (Android APK) and PC platforms. Built with a clean, decoupled architecture utilizing Finite State Machines (FSM), Object Pooling (BasePooling), and ScriptableObjects configuration, the game delivers responsive combat, intelligent 3-spread auto-aim shooting with charge management, tactical skills (timed AoE bombs, dash explosions, healing regeneration), distinct melee and ranged enemy AI behaviors with poison DoT mechanics, wave-based progression across multiple spawn nodes, and an EXP level-up stat growth system.

Gameplay Features

  • Player Combat & Triple-Shot Charge System: Fires 3 projectiles simultaneously in a fan spread (-15°, 0°, +15°) dealing 10 base damage per bullet. Features a 3-charge ammo pool with automatic regeneration (+1 charge/3s) and a 0.5s anti-spam buffer (PlayerShooting.cs, TripleShot.asset).
  • Intelligent Auto-Aim & Smooth Rotation: Scans targets via Physics.OverlapSphere, prioritizing the closest enemy in a 90° forward cone with automatic 360° fallback targeting, smoothly orienting the player at 180°/s (Quaternion.RotateTowards).
  • Tactical Active Skill Arsenal:
    • Skill 1 - Timed Bomb (ButtonBombSkill): Deploys an ordnance that detonates after a 2s delay, dealing 50 AoE damage in a 5-unit radius (12s cooldown).
    • Skill 2 - Dash Explosion (ButtonDashExplosionSkill): Quick 3-unit directional dash over 0.5s unleashing a 15 damage shockwave in a 3-unit radius (6s cooldown) with visual range indicators.
    • Skill 3 - Regeneration Heal (ButtonHealSkill): Restores 5% Max HP per second over 5s with FX_Healing particle feedback (20s cooldown).
  • FSM-Driven Enemy Archetypes:
    • Melee Enemy (Hugo / Minion): 220 HP, 3 unit/s speed; approaches player and executes a 45°-50° cone attack at 1.3 units dealing 30 damage via animation hitbox events with a 1s recovery delay (MeleeEnemyController.cs).
    • Ranged Enemy (Sandscorper / UFO): 180 HP, 2.7 unit/s speed; maintains a 3-unit distance and launches toxic projectiles (speed 10 unit/s, max 5 units) that apply a 30 DoT/s poison debuff for 3-4 ticks over 3s with duration refresh on re-hit (PlayerPoisonHandler.cs).
    • Large Minion (Elite Boss): High-HP boss summoned upon clearing all spawn point waves (SpawnLargeMinion.cs).
  • Wave Spawn & Node Progression: WaveSpawnManager cycles through map spawn points, generating 3–5 Melee and 1–3 Ranged enemies per wave; locks progression until all scene enemies are eliminated before transitioning to the next node and final boss wave.
  • EXP Progression & Dynamic Level-Up: Awards +30 EXP per kill; leveling up carries over excess EXP and scales stats: +40 Current & Max HP, +2 Armor, and +0.1 Damage Multiplier per level (PlayerLevel.cs, PlayerLevelGrowthData.asset).
  • Mathematical Damage & Defense Pipeline: Real-time damage mitigation formula (Damage Taken = Max(Raw Damage - Armor, 0)) and offense scaling (Damage Dealt = Base Damage * (1 + Damage Multiplier)).
  • Cross-Platform Dual Controls & HUD: Mobile Virtual Joystick (UIJoystick) with touch action buttons & cooldown countdowns alongside PC Keyboard (WASD/Arrow keys, J/K/L/Space/Shift hotkeys) + WorldSpace overhead enemy HP bars with camera auto-facing.

Technical Highlights

  • Finite State Machine (FSM) Architecture: Modular state decoupling for Player (Idle, Move, Shot) and Enemy archetypes (Idle, Move, Attack, Dead) orchestrated via StateManager and IState.
  • Modular LoadMonoBehaviour Framework: Custom architectural base class automating component binding and caching in Editor and Awake lifecycle to eliminate GetComponent overhead during gameplay.
  • Zero-Allocation Object Pooling (BasePooling): Generic pooling hierarchy managing Bullets, Bombs, Poison Projectiles, Melee/Ranged Enemies, Bosses, and VFX spheres, achieving 0 GC allocation during active combat.
  • Data-Driven Architecture (ScriptableObjects): 100% externalized configuration in Assets/Resources/ (PlayerSO, MeleeEnemySO, RangedEnemySO, TripleShot, BombData, DashExplosionData, HealSkillData, PlayerLevelGrowthData) for instant balance tuning without code recompilation.
  • Unity New Input System Integration: Leveraged UnityEngine.InputSystem with 2D Composite Vectors and cross-input blending between PC keyboard/mouse and Mobile touch UI.
  • Observer Pattern & UI Binding: Decoupled UI synchronization via IHealthObserver and IChargeObserver for smooth, reactive health bars and charge counters.

Challenges & Solutions

1. Zero-Allocation Combat with High Entity Density

Challenge: Continuous generation of 3-spread bullets, timed bomb explosions, poison projectiles, impact particles, and multi-node enemy waves triggered severe GC memory spikes and stutter.

Solution: Developed the BasePooling system for all combat actors and visual effects. Pre-warmed instances on startup and handled clean activation/deactivation cycles.

Result: Reduced gameplay runtime GC allocation to exactly 0 B/frame, ensuring a rock-solid 120+ FPS on PC and 60 FPS locked on Android devices.

2. Multi-Layered Damage Mitigation & Poison DoT System

Challenge: Harmonizing direct weapon impacts, flat armor reduction, percentage multipliers, and recurring poison DoT ticks (PlayerPoisonHandler) without tightly coupling game components.

Solution: Structured separate DamageSender and DamageReceiver components, while encapsulating DoT tick intervals and timer reset logic in dedicated coroutines.

Result: Transparent, extensible damage pipeline supporting dynamic level-up scaling and debuff stacking with accurate visual feedback.

3. Smooth Dual-Mode Auto-Aim & Target Acquisition

Challenge: Creating responsive auto-aim for mobile and PC controls that prioritizes immediate frontal threats without erratic snapping or failing when enemies flank from behind.

Solution: Engineered a two-tier spatial query using Physics.OverlapSphere: scanning a 90° forward cone first, falling back to a full 360° nearest-target sweep, then rotating the character smoothly at 180°/s using Quaternion.RotateTowards.

Result: Snappy, arcade-quality shooting controls with smooth rotation and zero targeting jitter.
